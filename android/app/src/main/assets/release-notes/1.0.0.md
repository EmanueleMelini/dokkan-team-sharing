First public release of DokkanTS Category Helper, an Android helper for Dokkan Battle JP.

- Type a category name in English, pick it, and the Japanese name goes straight into the game's category search, or onto your clipboard. A bubble over the game opens the search.
- Comes with the 99 categories of the game, with short aliases.
- English interface, in Italian when the phone is in Italian.
- Check for updates downloads the new categories from dokkants.app and adds the ones you do not have yet. It is the only time the app goes online.
- Dictionary editor: add, edit and delete categories, and import or export them as JSON.
- Needs Android 8.0 or newer. Free and unofficial: not affiliated with Bandai Namco Entertainment or Akatsuki.
