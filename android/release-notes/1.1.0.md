DokkanTS Category Helper 1.1.0 checks for new categories by itself.

- Automatic check for updates: at most once a day, when you open Dokkan and the bubble appears, the app downloads the list of categories from dokkants.app and adds the ones you do not have yet, just like Check for updates does. If it fails, it tries again the next time you open Dokkan, an hour later at the earliest.
- A new switch in the settings, Check for updates automatically, turns it off. It is on by default, and Check for updates still works either way.
- A short message appears over the game only when new categories are added. Nothing shows when the dictionary is already up to date or the check fails.
- The settings show when the last automatic check ran and how it went.
- The short messages over the game, like the one that asks you to paste a name yourself, now show on Android 13 and newer too.
